# Delivery Basics - Windows build

Download DeliveryBasics.zip, extract it, run DeliveryBasics.exe.

## Latest (2026-10-01, build 5)
- Voice chat: proximity voice in online games (loud up close, fades out ~60 m). [V] mutes your mic; a marker shows who is talking.
- Revive: wave a dollar bill at your downed friend - they gobble your hand, you shake them off and yeet them.
- Engineer: [Q] places a gadget, [R] picks it: snap trap, spring pad, glue mine, zapper, barricade, repair station (4 out at once). No more shop discount.
- Party Pooper now deals half damage (still knocks everything flat). Tank ground slam radius 7 m -> 4.5 m.
- AK-47 removed (refunded). Rocket Launcher is now a gun ($3000, chapter 3). Guns cost more: Shotgun $900, SMG $1300, Sniper $2200.
- Store stock unlocks by chapter (reputation 10 / 25).
- Style tips pay less, repeats pay less each time, capped per run, and tricks are harder to pull off.
- Fixes: rope lets go when you put it away; company van no longer flies; rocks no longer block you with invisible corners.

## Build 4
- Double Package modifier (B and up): two boxes, both must be signed for; pay graded on their average condition.
- Solo AI buddy: carries the other side of the box, guards it, revives you.
- Garage tab in the store: the Company Van (drives the box ~220 m up the road, once per run, on runs you host).
- Training dummy in the HQ yard: walk up to it and your gear comes out; shows last hit / DPS / total.
- Mastery alt fire: right mouse once an item has 2 mastery stars (special shots and swings; utilities overcharge).

## Build 3
- Mission board: click a job (or DETAILS) for a full-screen job sheet: the customer's (ridiculous) request, what every modifier really does, the package, the gang, the boss and the pay breakdown.
- Real models for the Gull, Mama Gull, Zipline Twins, Con Artist, the Skyhook Chopper (spinning rotors, horseshoe magnet) and the Mammoth Hauler semi.

## Build 2
- Thief numbers and boss health/firepower scale with crew size (2 players = baseline).
- New thieves: Gull, Zipline Twins, Con Artist. Bosses by gang: Skyhook Chopper, Mammoth Hauler, Mama Gull, Thief King.
- A-rank raids are Zipline Twin drops; fog bank weather roll.

## Build 1
- Clients get paid at the end of a job, package durability x2, crew auto-returns to the company.
